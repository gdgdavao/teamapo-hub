## Users

- **Attendees (Developers, Students, Tech Professionals in Davao & Region XI)**: Discover local tech events on mobile devices (often under bright daylight or while on transit). Need to register in under two minutes without account creation friction, upload GCash/Maya payment receipts reliably, present QR check-in tickets at venue doors, submit post-event feedback, and claim verifiable digital certificates for their professional portfolios.
- **GDG Davao Organizers & Volunteers**: Community organizers with limited volunteer time. Need rapid event publishing, multi-tier ticketing, instant queue-based payment verification, real-time venue check-in scanning on mobile cameras, automated reminder emails, and one-click bulk certificate generation.

## Product Purpose

A centralized, unified event lifecycle management platform engineered specifically for the GDG Davao community. TeamApo Hub eliminates fragmented toolsets (Google Forms, manual spreadsheet tracking, manual payment reconciliation, ad-hoc email reminders) by automating registration, ticket issuance, venue check-in, and credential verification into a single, cohesive, high-reliability system.

## Brand Personality

- **Welcoming**: Warm, community-centered, and accessible to first-time tech attendees and students.
- **Efficient**: Zero bloat, fast workflows, and direct paths to action that respect the user's time.
- **Polished**: Clean, modern craft inspired by the elegance of Lu.ma and Google Material 3; professional and trustworthy for sponsor-grade developer events.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered, dated academic and government conference portals.
- Generic SaaS clichés: endless identical card grids, side-stripe border callouts, unearned hero metric boxes, and gratuitous gradient text.
- Modal-heavy flows that disrupt mobile screens and break browser back-navigation.
- Heavy decorative animations that degrade performance or stall fast venue check-in lines.

## Design Principles

- **Frictionless by Default**: Eliminate every unnecessary hurdle. Attendees register in under two minutes without mandatory accounts; check-in volunteers verify attendees with a single camera scan in under a second.
- **Dignity in Utility**: Clean, uncluttered UI where content and key actions lead. Visual styling serves operational clarity rather than superficial decoration.
- **Venue-Resilient**: Designed for the chaotic realities of live events—bright tropical daylight, dim conference auditoriums, intermittent mobile data connections, and one-handed phone use.
- **Google Heritage, Local Warmth**: Thoughtfully apply the iconic Google brand palette as purposeful functional accents rather than garish decoration, paired with an inviting Davao community spirit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA compliance across all public and admin interfaces.
- Minimum 48px touch targets across all mobile inputs, buttons, payment uploaders, and QR scanners.
- High-contrast text and UI elements resilient to both outdoor sunlight and dark venue environments.
- Fully semantic form controls with inline validation, accessible screen reader cues, and graceful `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks.
