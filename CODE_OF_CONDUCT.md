# Code of Conduct

TeamApo Hub is a community project. Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, decisions, and behavior rather than people.
- Protect attendee privacy and never disclose credentials or personal data.
- Accept feedback and resolve disagreements in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, and deliberate exposure of
secrets or personal information are not acceptable. Report conduct concerns to
`support@gdgdavao.org`.
